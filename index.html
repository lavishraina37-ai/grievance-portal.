Help centre

Answers for raising and tracking a grievance.

01

What counts as a grievance?

A grievance is a concern about school services, facilities, safety, academic support, or any issue affecting your learning experience.

02

How quickly will I get a response?

Your grievance is reviewed within two working days. You can follow every status change from the My grievances page.

03

Need confidential support?

For urgent safety concerns, contact your school administrator directly or use the emergency support number provided by your school.
